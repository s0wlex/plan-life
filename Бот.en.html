Telegram

A tip, a quote and your numbers every morning

The bot picks a claim from the base matching your topics and sends it in the morning. In the evening it sends a short check-in. It never repeats what it has already shown.

Setup

Four steps

Create a bot with BotFather

Find @BotFather in Telegram, send /newbot, pick a name and a username. It will give you a token.

Paste the token

Open config.json and replace ВСТАВЬ_СВОЙ_ТОКЕН with your token.

Launch it

Double-click Запустить_бота.cmd. Keep the window open while the bot runs.

Hand over your settings

In the builder press “Bot settings”, copy the code and send it to the bot with /import. After that tips match your conditions exactly.

The bot runs as long as its window is open. It is not a server service. To keep receiving messages while your computer is off, put the Бот folder on a free host — Render.com or Railway.app, start command node bot.js, token via an environment variable.
Commands

All commands

CommandWhat it does
/startSwitch reminders on and show the plan
/stopSwitch reminders off
/tagsList of topics with checkboxes
/tags +protein,sleepThree tips on a given topic
/tag shoulderThree tips on a given topic
/whyWhat the A–D grades mean
/langBot language: Russian or English
/plan 2150 158 3Calories, protein, sessions per week
/time 7Time of the morning message
/checkinMark the day, keeps a streak

The bot understands synonyms: calories and food map to nutrition, muscles to protein, debt to money, partner to relationships, career to work, anxiety to mind, tidying to home.

Everything is calculated locally and no data is uploaded. The bot is a reminder, not a doctor: blood tests, diagnoses and treatment decisions belong to a specialist.
