How to start

Four steps

Check six spheres

Body, money, relationships, work, mind, daily life — seven questions each. Answer honestly, without glossing over anything.

Say how deep you want to go

Gently, seriously, rebuild, or everything at once. It decides how many spheres enter the plan and how much weekly effort is asked of you.

Get your roadmap

Weak spheres rise to the top, your life score is calculated, and actions are split into stages: a week, a month, three months, a year.

Build and save

The roadmap, body numbers, blood tests and selected advice go into one document. Export to Word or PDF, hand settings to the bot with one command.

Everything runs offline. Calculations happen in the browser and no data leaves your device. The only part that needs a network connection is the Telegram bot itself.

Everything is calculated locally and no data is uploaded. The bot is a reminder, not a doctor: blood tests, diagnoses and treatment decisions belong to a specialist.
